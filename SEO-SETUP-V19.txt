IMPORTANT AFTER YOUR FINAL DOMAIN IS CONNECTED

In Vercel:
Project -> Settings -> Environment Variables

Add:
NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL

Value example:
https://your-final-domain.com

Use your real Rush Track domain, with https:// and no trailing slash.
Then Redeploy.

Why:
This makes canonical URLs, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, Open Graph URLs and structured-data URLs use the final domain instead of the temporary Vercel address.

After deploy verify:
/robots.txt
/sitemap.xml
/manifest.webmanifest

Then submit the final-domain sitemap in Google Search Console.
